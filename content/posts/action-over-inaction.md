# Action Over Inaction

I have considered myself somewhat (honestly EXTREMELY) philosophical throughout my life. I think for most of it, this served as an advantage towards me. I think I pondered a lot of the questions that a lot of people my age didn't ponder growing up, and had a certain degree of emotional maturity whether I recognized it or not (this feels weird to even say because I don't like accepting compliments, and I've found emotional maturity consistently feels more like finding all the ways that you are NOT mature yet, but that is part of the process). Over the past few years/months, I have realized I have reached a breaking point with introspection to be honest. I used to think that in order to change, you need to have your head in the right place (hence the introspection), and while I think that is true and a step that most people miss completely, ultimately me THINKING things doesn't do anything at all. I can have epiphany every single day, but if I don't change my life, my life won't change. Simple as that.

## The Cycle

I am trying to fix this issue but I have noticed I fall into a cyclical pattern when trying to fix it in the sense that I try to fix this problem the way I have fixed every other problem ever in my life: by thinking my way through it (which is what I am doing right now by writing this mini essay, quite meta hypocritical indeed). I guess I don't even know what to do besides think, and I don't know what to do about this issue besides try to think of other things to do but think.

## The Answer

I think I found my answer, I need to stop writing this, stop thinking, and just completely turn off my brain.
